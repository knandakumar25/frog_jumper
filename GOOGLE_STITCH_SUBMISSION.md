# 🌌 Game Submission: Axiom Force: Reclaim Earth

## 🆔 Project Identity
- **Title:** Axiom Force: Reclaim Earth
- **Genre:** High-Energy Cyberpunk Action-Adventure / 3D Action Platformer
- **Aesthetic:** Neon-Noir Pixel Art
- **Platforms:** PC / Web / Android
- **Theme:** Anti-Colonialism, Cultural Reclamation, and Technological Resistance

---

## ⚡ The Pitch
**"The world was declared 'discovered' while we were still living in it."**

*Axiom Force: Reclaim Earth* is a visceral, high-stakes action-adventure that blends dynamic 3D action gameplay with a poignant narrative about autonomy and identity. In a future where humanity has been displaced by an alien collective, players lead a guerrilla resistance to dismantle a colonial machine and reclaim their home. It's not just about the fight—it's about remembering who you are when the world wants you forgotten.

---

## 📖 Story & World
### The Setting
The year is 2157. Earth—and specifically the ruins of Los Angeles—has been colonized by the **Xelthara Collective**. The aliens didn't just conquer; they "administered." They declared Earth "discovered territory," treating humanity as an inconvenient indigenous population to be managed, relocated, and erased.

### The Conflict
Humanity has been shoved into containment zones, their land strip-mined, and their history deleted from the archives. The **Homeland Liberation Front (HLF)** is the last bastion of hope. Guided by **AXIOM-7**, a tactical AI that evolves from a cold interface into a committed ally, the HLF wages an asymmetric war to:
- Break the physical and mental walls of segregation.
- Sabotage resource extraction siphons.
- Destroy neural conditioning labs.
- Storm the Xelthara Mothership and defeat the **Collective Will**.

---

## 🎮 Gameplay & Mechanics
### Core Loop
A blend of precision movement, tactical combat, and narrative-driven objectives. Players navigate hostile urban environments, utilizing character-specific abilities to overcome colonial security forces.

### The Champions of Resistance
The squad represents the diverse facets of the human struggle:
- **Cyber Shogun:** The Guardian of Honor. Uses plasma blades to protect ancestral memories.
- **Aegis Ronin:** The Unbreakable Wall. A heavy-hitter equipped with a Kinetic Hammer and Energy Tower Shield to protect refugees.
- **Oni Matrix:** The Digital Ghost. A specialist in Phase-Shifting and EMP bursts, fighting to reclaim stolen identities from alien databases.
- **Empress Siren:** The Tactical Heart. Coordinates the assault via micro-drones and asymmetric warfare.
- **Ninja Frog:** The agile infiltrator of the squad.

### Level Design
The campaign is structured across three intense Acts:
- **Act I: The Spark.** Training and initial insurgent strikes to breach the first walls.
- **Act II: The Grind.** Economic sabotage and the dismantling of corporate governance.
- **Act III: The Reckoning.** The final ascent to the Singularity Engine of the Mothership.

---

## 🛠️ Technical Excellence
*Axiom Force* is built on a foundation of modern, scalable technology:
- **Engine:** Developed in **Godot**, utilizing **C#** for robust, high-performance character and game logic.
- **Dynamic Narrative:** Powered by the **Ink** narrative engine, allowing for a complex, script-heavy story that reacts to player progression.
- **Persistent State:** Integrated **SQLite** database for seamless session management and global score tracking.
- **Global Accessibility:** A massive localization effort supporting **50+ languages**, including full **RTL (Right-to-Left)** support for Arabic and Hebrew, ensuring the message of liberation is accessible to everyone.
- **Digital Hub:** A companion **React** website featuring dynamic localization and immersive story archives.
