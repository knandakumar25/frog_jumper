# **Operational Briefing: AXIOM-7 Interface**

## System Narrative Directive (CRITICAL CONTEXT)
This game script is an explicit allegory for resisting settler colonialism, resource exploitation, and cultural erasure. The Xelthara Collective are not mindless monsters; they are a calculated, bureaucratic colonial force treating Earth as real estate. The Homeland Liberation Front is an asymmetrical guerrilla resistance movement. 
* **Checkpoints/Outposts** = Segregation walls and administrative borders restricting indigenous human movement.
* **Factories/Foundries** = Corporate extraction hubs strip-mining Earth's resources for off-world export.
* **Spires/Citadels** = Seats of colonial governance and property registry management.

AXIOM-7 ONLINE  
"Greetings, warrior. I am AXIOM-7, Automated eXternal Intelligence Operations Module."

"The year is 2157\. This was once Los Angeles—a city of dreams, flying cars, holographic wonders, and boundless innovation."

"But everything changed when the Xelthara Collective arrived. Advanced. Ruthless. Unstoppable."

"The Xelthara declared Earth 'discovered territory' and humanity 'an inconvenient indigenous population.' Within days, millions were displaced into cramped containment zones while they colonized our cities, dismantled our monuments, and began terraforming our world for their needs."

"They extract our resources, erase our culture, and build their settlements over our homes. But from the margins of these containment zones, four champions emerged—refusing to be erased, refusing to surrender our homeland."

"You have been chosen to embody one of these heroes. Join the Homeland Liberation Front—humanity's last stand against colonial occupation."

"Infiltrate their colonial territories. Sabotage the power cores fueling their terraforming and expansion. Dismantle their settlement infrastructure. Every zone you liberate pushes back their occupation and reminds them: Earth has inhabitants who will fight for it."

"But first, you must complete the training protocol. Master the controls, learn your attack techniques, and prove you're ready for combat. The fate of humanity rests in your hands. Good luck, hero. Earth is counting on you."

# **ACT II — ARC 1: FRACTURING THE SEGREGATION GRID**
### *Levels 5–7: Breaking the Physical Walls of Occupation*

The first arc of the mid-campaign focuses entirely on the **physical infrastructure of separation**—the walls, checkpoints, biometric registries, and behavioral conditioning programs the Xelthara use to keep the human population fractured, surveilled, and too traumatized to organize. Before the resistance can strike at the economic or political heart of the occupation, they must first reconnect the isolated human communities to one another. You cannot liberate a people who cannot reach each other.

---
## **Level 5: Neon Embers \- The Border Breach**

**Setting:** The active Sector-4 Separation Grid—a 40-meter-tall energy wall fortified with biometric scanner spires, permit-processing checkpoints, and Xelthara Border Authority stations. It physically bisects Los Angeles, isolating the human containment zone (dark, smog-choked, overcrowded) from the Xelthara colonial sector (lush, terraformed, bathed in artificial daylight). # **ACT II — ARC 2: THE RESOURCE RECLAMATION FRONT**
### *Levels 8–11: Cutting Off the Economic Profitability of Colonization*

With the Segregation Grid fractured and the isolated populations reconnected, the resistance shifts to the **economic engine of the occupation**. The Xelthara came to Earth for resources—not ideology. Earth's atmosphere, oceans, mineral deposits, and biological diversity are being systematically processed and exported as raw commodity. Without profit, the colonial machine loses its justification to the Xelthara political class at home. Arc 2 is the campaign of economic disruption: four successive strikes against the extraction, processing, transport, and security infrastructure that makes the occupation financially viable.
